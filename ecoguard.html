EcoGuard

Turning environmental awareness into practical change within my school community.

From awareness
to action

Through EcoGuard, I worked with my school community to promote more sustainable everyday practices. One project focused on reducing unnecessary plastic packaging in the school cafeteria and encouraging the transition toward biodegradable alternatives.

Small changes can become visible changes.

Rather than thinking about sustainability only as a large-scale environmental problem, I wanted to explore how everyday decisions within a community could also contribute to a more sustainable campus.

The project

A collection of moments from EcoGuard activities, campaigns, discussions, and environmental initiatives.

What I learned

Environmental change does not always begin with large scientific projects. Sometimes it begins with a conversation, a different choice, or a small change in the environment immediately around us. EcoGuard gave me an opportunity to experience environmental action from a community perspective.
