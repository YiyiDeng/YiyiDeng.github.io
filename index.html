About me

I became interested in environmental science by asking a simple question:

How can we understand change in nature at a scale too large to observe directly?

My interest in environmental science began with observing landscapes and wildlife. Over time, that curiosity led me to maps, satellite imagery, and ecological modeling.

Today, I am especially interested in biodiversity conservation under climate change and in using geospatial methods to connect environmental data with ecological questions.

Six-day expedition

A six-day hiking expedition from Huizhou to Hong Kong — one of the experiences that taught me how to keep moving when the trail becomes difficult.

Six days. One trail. A lot of uncertainty.

Before the expedition, I was more worried about whether I could finish than excited about the journey. Six days of walking, fatigue, changing conditions, and teamwork eventually taught me that resilience is not just an ecological concept — it is something people practice too.
