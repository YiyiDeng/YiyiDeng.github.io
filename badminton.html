BEYOND RESEARCH · SPORT

Badminton

A sport that keeps me moving, brings people together, and creates memories I will always carry with me.

01 · WHY I PLAY

More than a sport

Badminton has become a meaningful part of my life. What I enjoy most is not simply the game itself, but everything that comes with it: staying active, challenging myself, meeting people, and sharing the excitement of playing together. Through regular practice and time on court, badminton has gradually become a rewarding part of my everyday routine. It reminds me that some of the best experiences come from doing something you genuinely enjoy with others.

02 · DAILY TRAINING

On the court

Beyond team photographs and competitions, much of my experience with badminton comes from ordinary training sessions. The repeated drills, focused practice, and time spent on court have become a familiar and rewarding part of my routine. Each session gives me a chance to stay active, improve, and enjoy the game.

03 · OUR TEAM

Better together

Some of my most treasured badminton memories come from being part of our school team. From training sessions to shared moments on and off the court, I have come to appreciate the friendships built through a common passion. We encourage one another, celebrate small improvements, and work towards becoming better together. These experiences have made badminton much more than an individual sport for me.

04 · WHAT BADMINTON GAVE ME

More than I expected

Looking back, I realize that the most valuable things I have gained from badminton go far beyond the sport itself.

A LITTLE REFLECTION

What I will remember most is not just how I played, but the people I played alongside.

Badminton has given me more than a healthy body. It has brought me lasting friendships, meaningful memories, and the experience of working hard towards something together. The best moments are often the ones we share: encouraging each other, pushing through challenges, celebrating progress, and enjoying every game. These are the memories that make me want to keep coming back to the court.
