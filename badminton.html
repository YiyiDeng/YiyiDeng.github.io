Badminton

More than a Duke of Edinburgh's Award requirement — badminton became a sport I chose to keep returning to.

Why I play

Badminton has been part of my life both inside and outside the Duke of Edinburgh's Award. The award gave me a structured reason to train, but over time the sport became something I enjoyed independently — a way to stay active, challenge myself, and spend time with other people.

Beyond the award

Duke of Edinburgh's Award introduced badminton as part of my physical recreation, but it does not define my relationship with the sport. I continue to play because I enjoy the process — the movement, competition, concentration, and the feeling of gradually getting better.

I did not keep playing badminton simply because I had to. I kept playing because I wanted to.
