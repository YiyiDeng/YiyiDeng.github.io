RESEARCH · 01 · BIODIVERSITY & CLIMATE

Climate resilience of flagship bird communities

A comparative study of how climate change may reshape the spatial resilience of flagship bird communities in China and the United States.

Research question

How does climate change influence the spatial resilience of flagship bird communities in China and the United States?

Why this question?

Climate change is reshaping species distributions and ecological communities. However, species richness alone does not fully describe whether a community can remain resilient when environmental conditions change. This project therefore examines community resilience by combining species distributions with functional redundancy and response diversity.

Occurrence GBIF

Methodology

Species distribution models were first developed to estimate the environmental suitability of each species. Community resilience was then quantified using functional redundancy and response diversity.

Species occurrence ↓ MaxEnt species distribution models ↓ Functional Redundancy + Response Diversity ↓ Avian Community Resilience ↓ XGBoost + SHAP
Model MaxEnt
Resilience FR + RD
Machine Learning XGBoost
