RESEARCH · 03 · SPECIES DISTRIBUTION

Species distribution and niche conservatism of Yunnan primates

Using species distribution modeling to explore environmental suitability and ecological niche patterns among threatened primate species in Yunnan.

The question

Where are suitable habitats for different primate species, and to what extent do closely related species occupy similar ecological niches?

Study species

Nomascus concolor

Black crested gibbon

A threatened primate species whose distribution is closely associated with suitable forest habitat.

Trachypithecus crepusculus

Indochinese lutung

A primate species used to investigate differences in environmental suitability and ecological niche.

Modeling framework

Species occurrence records were combined with environmental variables to estimate habitat suitability using Maximum Entropy species distribution modeling.

Species occurrence data ↓ Environmental variables ↓ MaxEnt ↓ Habitat suitability ↓ Ecological niche comparison
