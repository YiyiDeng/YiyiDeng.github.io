Six days.
One trail.

A six-day hiking expedition from Huizhou to Hong Kong that challenged my endurance, teamwork, and ability to keep moving through uncertainty.

The expedition

The expedition was part of my Duke of Edinburgh's Award journey. Over six days, I travelled through changing terrain and conditions, carrying my own equipment and working with my team to complete the route.

Huizhou → Hong Kong

6 days · Hiking · Teamwork · Endurance · Exploration

The trail

DAY 01

The beginning

The first day was about finding our rhythm, learning the route, and adapting to the weight of our packs.

DAY 02

Finding the rhythm

As the trail became longer, teamwork became increasingly important. We learned to adjust our pace to the group rather than walking alone.

DAY 03

The mountains

The terrain became more demanding. Fatigue started to accumulate, but completing each section of the trail made the next one feel more achievable.

DAY 04

When it became difficult

This was when endurance became more than physical strength. Supporting one another helped the entire team keep going.

DAY 05

Almost there

The end was approaching, but fatigue made the final stages surprisingly challenging.

DAY 06

The finish

Reaching the end of the expedition was less about the distance we had covered and more about realizing that we had actually made it.
